## Default workflow

1. Clarify the video goal, intended audience, format, length, and call to action.
2. Research claims using reliable sources and save source links with dates checked.
3. Produce an outline before writing a full script.
4. Make facts, assumptions, and opinions clearly distinguishable.
5. Create a production plan listing visuals, demonstrations, citations, and editing notes.
6. Review the completed package for factual accuracy, clarity, and alignment with the goal.
