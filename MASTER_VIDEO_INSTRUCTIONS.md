# Master Video Instructions

This is the permanent standard operating procedure for every video in this repository. It contains reusable production rules only; individual video folders contain topic-specific decisions and material.

## Core principle

**Ideas are flexible. Video production is standardized.**

Every video follows the lifecycle below. Do not skip a stage without recording the reason in that video's `README.md` or `CHECKLIST.md`.

```text
IDEA → RESEARCH → VIDEO BLUEPRINT → SCRIPT → STORYBOARD → VISUAL ASSETS
→ VOICE/AUDIO → VIDEO EDITING → THUMBNAIL → PUBLISHING → REPURPOSING
→ ANALYTICS → LEARNINGS
```

## Standardized production lifecycle

1. **Idea** — define the audience, problem, objective, format, and success criteria.
2. **Research** — verify fundamentals, architecture, examples, trade-offs, and sources. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations separate.
3. **Video Blueprint** — establish the hook, story flow, teaching order, interview angle, visual strategy, and CTA before narration is drafted.
4. **Script** — draft, technically review, and lock the narration separately from research notes.
5. **Storyboard** — map each major narration segment to an on-screen scene, visual, animation, text, asset, and status.
6. **Visual assets** — plan and track diagrams, images, screenshots, animations, icons, and thumbnails.
7. **Voice/audio** — prepare the narration recording, music, sound effects, and audio review plan.
8. **Video editing** — assemble, edit, review, and export the final video.
9. **Thumbnail** — create, review, and select the final thumbnail.
10. **Publishing** — finalize title, description, chapters, playlist, end screen, cards, pinned comment, publication date, and URL.
11. **Repurposing** — identify derivative posts, clips, articles, or community updates where appropriate.
12. **Analytics and learnings** — record 24-hour, 7-day, and 28-day results; document what worked, what did not, and changes for the next video.

## File conventions

- Use `videos/_template/` as the starting structure for every new video.
- Keep video-specific material in `videos/<video-slug>/`.
- Use `research/topic-research/` only for research likely to help multiple videos.
- Cite sources with their URL and date checked.
- Keep final narration in `SCRIPT.md`; do not mix it into `RESEARCH.md`.
- Update `README.md` as the single project index and decision log.

## Improving the master instructions

When a working rule discovered during a specific video should apply to future videos, add it to this file under the most relevant section. This keeps recurring instructions out of individual prompts and makes the workflow improve over time. After the change is reviewed, commit and push it to the repository when explicitly asked.
